# Mama-Lee's Bakery

A modern bakery website designed and developed for a **real-world client**.

The project was created to give the business a professional online presence, showcase its products, and provide customers with an easy way to discover and connect with the bakery.

> **Client Project** — Private Repository

---

## About

**Mama-Lee's Bakery** is a responsive business website designed around the needs and identity of a local bakery.

The website focuses on presenting the bakery's products in an attractive and accessible way while creating a professional digital presence that can be used by customers to learn more about the business.

This project was developed as a real client engagement rather than a purely personal or tutorial project.

---

## Project Goals

The primary goals of the project were to:

* Establish a professional online presence
* Showcase the bakery and its products
* Create a visually appealing customer experience
* Make the website responsive across different devices
* Provide clear and accessible business information
* Create a foundation that can be expanded as the business grows
