Hello, I'm Sarah

The sweet heart and baking engineer behind Sweet Crumbs Bakery. I started baking in my cozy Pretoria kitchen on weekends, driven by a deep love for structural crumb precision, fluffy buttercreams, and the smiles of family tasting warm recipes from scratch.

Before it became a brand, baking was my mindfulness practice. Every egg cracked, every sugar rose molded, and every custom texture whipped was an act of quiet love. Today, alongside a tiny team of passionate sweet assistants, we handcraft custom cakes, pristine cupcakes, box cookies, and luxury desserts specifically crafted for Pretoria's most beautiful memories and daily happy celebrations.
